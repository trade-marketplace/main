You pay when you accept your price, before anyone turns up. That locks in your booking.

Your tradie gets paid after you’ve checked the job and said it’s all good (or 48 hours after they finish, if we don’t hear from you).

## What if there’s extra work?

You’ll see photos and the extra cost first. You only pay for it if you say yes.
