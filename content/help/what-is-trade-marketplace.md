Trade Marketplace connects people who need a job done with local people who can do it. You post the job, you get one fixed price, and someone near you takes it and gets it done.

It covers licensed trades like electrical, plumbing, gas and building, and everyday jobs like moving, cleaning, gardening and assembly.
